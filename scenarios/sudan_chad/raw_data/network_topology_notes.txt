Sudan-Chad FLEE topology revision

The initial province-level network used a single border transit point (Adre)
connected directly to aggregate Ouaddai, Sila and Wadi_Fira destination proxies.

A uniform-capacity diagnostic showed substantial spatial allocation error:
Ouaddai: 214323 simulated vs 319545 observed
Sila: 106613 simulated vs 63808 observed
Wadi Fira: 91396 simulated vs 28983 observed

UNHCR evidence indicates that displacement into eastern Chad occurred through
more than 32 border entry points and that refugees were distributed across
spontaneous settlements, transit locations and multiple camps.

Therefore the single-Adre three-province topology is treated as a simplified
diagnostic model, not the final historical validation network.
